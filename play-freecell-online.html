The responsive card table works on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers. Tap a card to send it to a valid destination, or use a hint when you cannot spot the next move.

How to play FreeCell

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, building each suit from ace to king. On the tableau, build downward in alternating colors. Each of the four free cells can temporarily hold one card.

Quick strategy tips

  • Uncover aces and low cards early.
  • Keep free cells available whenever possible.
  • Opening an empty column gives you valuable room.
  • Plan several moves ahead before filling a free cell.
